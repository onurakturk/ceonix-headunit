Privacy Policy for CEONIX Headunit

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CEONIX Headunit and its companion app CEONIX Setup ("we", "our", or "us") handle your data when you use our Android applications.

1. Summary

CEONIX Headunit has no servers and no account system. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to any third party. Everything described below stays on your own devices.

2. What the app processes, and where it goes

The application works as a display and input receiver for your Android phone via the Android Auto protocol. All content shown (maps, music, contacts, messages) remains on your connected phone and is processed by the Android Auto app installed on that phone, subject to Google's own privacy policies.

* Location: The head unit's GPS position and speed are sent to your own connected phone, over the direct link between the two devices, so that Android Auto navigation can use them. They also drive the Ride screen (speed, lean angle, g-force, altitude, sunset time). The most recent position is kept on the head unit so that the Ride screen and the automatic day/night mode work before a new GPS fix arrives.
* Ride telemetry: While you ride, the app writes sensor readings (speed, lean angle, g-force) to files in its own storage area on the head unit. These files are used for diagnostics, are rotated automatically, are never uploaded, and are removed when the app is uninstalled.
* Microphone: Passed directly to the connected phone for Android Auto voice control and hands-free calls. No audio is recorded or stored by CEONIX Headunit.
* Diagnostic logs: Only if you switch log capture on in the developer settings, the app writes its own log output to a file on the head unit. Nothing is uploaded; the file leaves the device only if you export or share it yourself.
* Settings backup: If you export your settings, a file is written to the Downloads folder of the head unit. It stays there until you delete it.

3. Permissions

The app requests permissions solely for its core functionality:

* Bluetooth and Nearby Wi-Fi devices: Used to find your phone and to set up the direct Wi-Fi link that carries Android Auto.
* Location: See section 2.
* Microphone: See section 2.
* Display over other apps and running in the background: Used so that Android Auto can come up on its own when your phone connects.
* Notifications: Used to show the state of the connection while the app runs in the background.

4. CEONIX Setup (phone app)

CEONIX Setup is a one-time setup tool. It scans for nearby Wi-Fi networks to find the head unit's Wi-Fi Direct address and sends that address to the head unit you select, over Bluetooth. Android requires the Location permission for Wi-Fi scanning; the app does not read your position. The scan results are shown on screen and are not stored or sent anywhere else.

5. Third-Party Services

CEONIX Headunit does not integrate any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking tools.
